Raqoon Travel eSIM Refund Policy



1. Scope and mandatory consumer rights

This policy is subject to mandatory consumer rights and Turkish-law baseline protections.

2. Unused and uninstalled profile cancellation

A customer may request cancellation of an unused, uninstalled profile, subject to applicable law and the order terms.

3. Installed, activated, or used profile technical review

For an installed, activated or used profile, Raqoon may request technical evidence and obtain a third-party provisioning review.

4. Non-refundable circumstances permitted by law

A refund may be unavailable only where permitted by applicable law, including digital-service circumstances after lawful performance begins.

5. Request channel, evidence, and review outcome

Submit the request through the published support channel with order details and relevant evidence. The outcome follows the review and mandatory law.

6. Chargebacks and payment-provider rules

Chargebacks and any payment-provider procedures follow the confirmed provider’s rules; no provider is identified until verified.

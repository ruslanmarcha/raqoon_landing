Raqoon eSIM Refund Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026
Seller: Qat Bilişim ve Yazılım Teknolojileri Ltd. Şti., Ankara, Türkiye
Requests: help@raqoon.app

1. Scope and mandatory rights

This policy applies to Raqoon eSIM purchases and top-ups. It does not exclude rights that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law. Where a mandatory rule gives you a broader remedy, that rule applies.

2. Digital delivery and express request to begin performance

An eSIM is a digital, prepaid product delivered electronically. Before checkout, we will ask you to acknowledge the immediate delivery of the activation details and, where required, expressly request that performance begin before any withdrawal period ends. We keep a record of that acknowledgement, the delivery time, profile status and usage information.

3. When a refund may be available

Contact us promptly if the profile was not delivered, cannot be installed or activated because of a confirmed technical fault, the delivered plan materially differs from the order, or the device was incompatible despite our product information being materially incorrect. We will first investigate and, where appropriate, offer a workable replacement, correction, credit or refund in accordance with applicable law.

4. When a refund is normally unavailable

To the extent permitted by law, a refund is normally unavailable after the eSIM profile has been installed, activated, connected to a network, topped up or used, or after digital performance has begun with the acknowledgement described above. It is also normally unavailable for unused allowance after plan expiry, a change of travel plans, an unlocked-device requirement that was disclosed, an incorrect installation by the customer, unsupported use, or loss of QR-code confidentiality caused by the customer.

5. How to make a request

Send the request from the email used for the order to help@raqoon.app with your order number, a clear description of the issue, device model and version, destination, installation/activation screenshots where relevant, and the time the issue occurred. Do not send passport copies, full payment-card data or bank statements. We may request proportionate technical information required to investigate the specific problem.

6. Review and payment

We verify delivery, installation, activation, usage and network-provisioning status with E7 Access where necessary. We may decline a request that is fraudulent, abusive, duplicated or outside the applicable legal remedy. If a refund is approved, it is returned to the original payment method where technically possible, within the period required by applicable law and payment-provider rules.

7. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a payment dispute so we can investigate the issue. This does not limit your statutory rights or your right to contact your payment provider. We may provide order and technical fulfilment records to the payment provider to respond to a chargeback.
